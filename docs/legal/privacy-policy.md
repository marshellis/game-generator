## The short version
Parents make the account. Kids get a profile with only a nickname, an avatar and a birth year.
We do not show ads, sell data, or share kids' information for marketing. Parents can see,
download and delete everything about their child at any time.

## What we collect
**From parents:** email address and password (or sign-in method), household name, payment
details (handled by our payment provider; we never see full card numbers), subscription status.
**For each child profile (entered by the parent):** a nickname (not a real name), an avatar,
birth year (we use it only to pick the right difficulty), play and learning progress (which
skills were practised, how well), time played, high scores.
**Automatically:** basic technical logs (needed to keep the service running and secure).
We do **not** collect children's email, real name, photo, voice, precise location, contacts
or device advertising IDs. Please do not put personal details in nicknames.

## How we use it
To run the games, save progress and scores, choose learning activities suited to the child, apply
the limits parents set, process subscriptions, keep the service safe, and fix problems.
We do not use children's information to advertise to them or build advertising profiles.

## Who we share it with
Only service providers that run the service for us, under their own terms: web hosting and
database [Vercel, Upstash], payments [Stripe and app-store billing], game servers [Fly.io].
We do not sell information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.
Leaderboards show only nickname, avatar and score; parents can turn this off.

## Children's privacy (US COPPA)
We collect information from a child only through a parent's account, after the parent consents.
Parents may at any time review, download, correct or delete their child's information, or refuse
further collection, from the parent settings page or by contacting us. Deleting a profile
deletes its data from our systems [within X days; backups within Y days].

## Keeping it safe and how long we keep it
Data is encrypted in transit; access is limited to the people who run the service. We keep a
child's data until the parent deletes the profile or the account, or after [X months] of
inactivity. We will tell parents if a breach affects their family.

## Changes
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we tell parents by email before it applies and
ask for fresh consent if we start collecting anything new about children.
